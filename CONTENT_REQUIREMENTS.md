## 5. General Writing Style

All website content should be:

- Professional
- Clear
- Student-friendly
- Parent-friendly
- International in tone
- Trustworthy
- Ethical
- Informative
- Easy to scan
- Free from exaggerated claims
- Free from unnecessary jargon
- Written in Indian English unless otherwise approved

### Preferred Writing Characteristics

- Short paragraphs
- Clear headings
- Direct explanations
- Helpful examples
- Action-oriented buttons
- Transparent limitations
- Accurate terminology
- Simple explanations of complex processes

### Avoid

- Excessive promotional language
- Artificial urgency
- Fear-based marketing
- Unverified statistics
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Misleading claims
- Very long paragraphs
- Repeated slogans
- Copying competitor content
