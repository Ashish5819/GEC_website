## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document records important decisions made during the planning, design, development, security, content, deployment, and maintenance of the Global Education & Careers website.

It is intended to:

- Preserve the history of major decisions
- Distinguish confirmed decisions from proposed decisions
- Prevent accidental changes
- Record the reason behind each decision
- Identify unresolved matters
- Track approvals
- Support future developers and reviewers
- Reduce confusion when requirements change
- Ensure that dummy information remains clearly marked
- Provide a reliable project reference

This document should not be used to silently replace earlier decisions.

When a decision changes, a new entry should be added that references the earlier decision.

---

## 2. Decision Status Definitions

### Confirmed

The decision has been explicitly approved, supplied by the owner, or accepted as the current project-planning direction and does not require the dummy marker. A confirmed planning decision does not by itself mean that the related service, office, partnership, system, or business operation is already active.

### Proposed

The decision is a realistic recommendation but has not yet been approved.

Every proposed decision must end with:

(D)

### Pending

The project requires an answer, approval, verification, or external dependency before the decision can be completed.

A realistic proposed option may be included and must end with:

(D)

### Rejected

The option has been reviewed and intentionally not selected.

### Superseded

The decision was previously valid but has been replaced by a later recorded decision.

### Deferred

The decision is intentionally postponed to a later phase or version.
