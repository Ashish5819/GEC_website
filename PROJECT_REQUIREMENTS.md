## 1. Project Overview

Global Education & Careers, abbreviated as GEC, is a proposed overseas education and career consultancy based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

The organisation is currently in the planning and pre-launch stage. It has not yet started business operations and is not yet legally registered.

The website will establish the initial digital presence of GEC and present its proposed services to students, parents, working professionals, corporate professionals, universities, and institutional partners.

The website will provide information about study destinations, universities, courses, applications, scholarships, visas, international careers, pre-departure preparation, and post-arrival support.

The website will also collect counselling enquiries, support appointment booking, promote educational events, and provide a foundation for future digital services.
